# Anti-AI-slop

## Banned

- Purple-to-blue gradient backgrounds.
- Emoji as functional icons.
- Generic centered hero over a gradient.
- Three identical rounded feature cards.
- Neon-on-dark cyber palette.
- AI-drawn SVG illustrations or silhouettes.
- Inter or Roboto as display type on Apple platforms.
- Endless glass cards without hierarchy.

## Required

- One warm accent on a neutral, intentional background.
- SF Symbols or a real custom icon set.
- Serif or strong display type for screen titles.
- Clear 3+ level type hierarchy.
- 8pt spacing grid.
- One signature detail per screen.
- Real content or honest placeholders.
