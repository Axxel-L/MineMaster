# Typography and color

## Type scales

- Display: 28 to 40pt, serif, bold.
- Heading: 20 to 24pt, semibold.
- Body: 15 to 17pt, regular.
- Caption: 10 to 13pt, secondary, monospace when technical.

## Color

- One warm accent.
- Neutral background with a warm undertone.
- Semantic colors for rarity, state, and destructive actions.
- Define every color as a token. No literals in views.
- Check contrast in dark mode.
